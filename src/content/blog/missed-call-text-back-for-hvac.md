When your phone rings and every tech is on a job, the caller does not leave a voicemail and wait. They search again and call the next company. A missed call text back fixes that by sending a text to the caller within seconds, from your business number, so the conversation starts before they move on.

## How it works

1. Someone calls your business number and nobody picks up.
2. Within a few seconds, the caller receives a text message from that same number.
3. They reply, and the system asks a few short questions: what is wrong, what is the address, and when do they need someone.
4. You get a summary on your phone, or the job is booked straight into your calendar.

The caller never has to call back, and you never have to hunt through voicemails at the end of the day.

## What the first text should say

Keep it short, name your company, and ask one easy question. HVAC has an obvious one: is the system not cooling or heating, or is it something else?

> Sorry we missed your call, this is [Company]. Is your system not cooling or heating, or is it something else? We can get you on the schedule.

A few rules that work for any trade:

- **Say who you are.** People ignore texts from numbers they do not recognize.
- **Ask one question.** A long message with three questions gets fewer replies than a short one with one.
- **Sound like a person.** Skip phrases like "Your inquiry is important to us."
- **Offer a next step.** Booking a visit beats "we will call you back."

## Handle emergencies differently

A no-heat call in January is not the same as a spring tune-up. Decide ahead of time which words mean "urgent" (no heat, no cooling in extreme weather, gas smell, water leaking from the unit) and have those messages page your on-call tech right away. Routine requests can wait for the next open slot.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Texting people who did not call you.** Only reply to inbound callers who contacted you first.
- **Ignoring opt-outs.** If someone replies STOP, the system must stop messaging them. This is a legal requirement, not just good manners.
- **Setting it and forgetting it.** Read a few real conversations each month and adjust the wording.
- **No person behind it.** A customer should always be able to reach a human when they want one.
